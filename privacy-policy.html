Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7/27/2026 · Effective for: SMART DUKA ERP SYSTEM

This Privacy Policy explains how LEONIA SANITARY LTD ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use SaviaSmart ERP (the "Service"). By creating an account or using the Service, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

  • Business name
  • Email address
  • Password (stored in hashed form)

1.2 Business & Inventory Data

  • Product, category, brand, and supplier records you enter
  • Stock quantities, reorder levels, and pricing
  • Sales transactions, payment methods, and store records

1.3 Usage Data

  • Log data such as access times, pages viewed, and actions taken
  • Device and browser information

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the Service
  • To generate reports, analytics, and AI-powered recommendations
  • To communicate with you about your account or updates to the Service
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or fraud
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. AI Advisor & Third-Party Processing

The AI Advisor feature may send business data you provide (such as inventory levels and sales summaries) to Anthropic in order to generate responses. This data is processed solely to answer your queries and is not used to train models unless otherwise stated by the provider's own terms.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your personal or business data. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the Service (e.g. hosting, analytics)
  • Authorities, when required by law or to protect our legal rights
  • A successor entity in the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale

5. Data Storage & Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain account and business data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the Service. Upon a verified deletion request, we delete your account login credentials. Business and financial records (e.g. sales, inventory, and transaction history) are retained after account deletion for accounting, audit, and legal compliance purposes, as described in Section 7.

7. Your Rights

  • Access, correct, or update your personal information
  • Request deletion of your account login credentials. Business and financial records associated with your account are retained after deletion for accounting, audit, and legal compliance purposes and are not deleted upon request
