The shape of the day

A day, from opening the shutter to locking up.

Nothing here asks you to change how you trade. It follows the way the day already goes.

Morning

Open the till

Whoever is on the counter writes down the money they are starting with. One person, one till.

Through the day

Sell, and the shelf follows

Every sale takes the item off the shelf count at the same moment. Nobody has to remember to do it twice.

When a regular asks

Give credit with a record

Put it on their name. Going past what you set for them needs a manager, and it is written down.

Evening

Count, and compare

Count the drawer. The difference is recorded whether it is short, over, or exactly right.

The drawer

Most systems tell you what you sold.

The harder question is whether the money is actually there. That is the one a notebook can never answer honestly, because a notebook can be rewritten after the fact.

01 One till, one person, one shift

Two people cannot both be responsible for the same drawer at the same time.

02 The sum is done for you

Opening money, plus cash sales, plus debts repaid in cash, less refunds. Then you count.

03 A short day stays short

Closing a shift cannot go back and change how it opened. That is the only reason the record is worth having.

Questions

The things shopkeepers ask first.

Do I need to buy any equipment?

No. It runs in the browser on the phone or computer you already have. If you own a barcode scanner it will work, and if you do not, you can search for a product by name instead.

What happens when the internet goes down?

You keep selling for cash, and those sales are held on the device and sync when the line returns. Your stock list and prices stay readable the whole time. Credit sales and card payments need the connection, because both have to be checked against something the device cannot see on its own. The till tells you so rather than failing quietly.

Can my staff see everything?

Only what you give them. A cashier sees the counter they are working. A manager sees their branch. The whole business is yours alone, and changing what someone can reach takes effect on their next action, not their next sign-in.

Is my data mine?

Yes. You can download a full copy of your business (products, sales, customers) as a file, at any time, without asking us.

Does it handle VAT?

If you are registered, yes: prices are VAT-inclusive the way they are quoted in Tanzanian retail, tax is set per product, and the return reconciles to what the drawer actually took. If you are not registered, none of it appears and nothing changes.

How far along is it?

In use by a small number of shops while we work closely with them. We would rather say that than claim a number we have not earned.
